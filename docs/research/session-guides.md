## Usability session for young people (40 minutes)

| Minutes | Part | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Welcome | Introductions, check the trusted adult is present or nearby, consent (young person and, for under 16s, parent), access needs, permission to record. |
| 5 | Warm-up | Something you'd change about your area or school, and who you'd tell. |
| 25 | Tasks (pick 3 or 4) | 1. Find a way to take part that fits your age and nation. 2. Find who to talk to if you're worried about something. 3. Use a tooltip to find out what an MYP is. 4. Find a free coding club. 5. Change the explainer to suit you. 6. Find how to suggest an idea. |
| 5 | Wrap-up | Easiest and hardest thing; one thing to change. Thank you, and how you'll hear what changed. |

Record: completed (yes, with help, no), the words people used and where they hesitated. Don't help unless asked or stuck for 2 minutes.

## Accessibility session (45 to 60 minutes)

| Minutes | Part | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Welcome and set-up | Consent; the person shows their set-up; agree how they'll share what they hear or see; interpreter introductions. |
| 35 | Tasks | Three tasks from the usability session, using their own technology or language. Note every barrier and workaround. |
| 10 | Reflection | What would make the biggest difference? Anything that would stop you using it? |

Severity: blocks (cannot complete), slows (completes with effort), annoys. Blockers become must-fix items straight away.

## Co-design workshop in a school or youth group (75 minutes)

| Minutes | Part | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Welcome | Ground rules agreed together; consent; how ideas will be used and credited (first names or nicknames only, if wanted). |
| 15 | The problem | Share what we've learned so far; agree the need in one sentence in the group's own words. |
| 25 | Ideas | Sketch, write, draw or act out ideas; share and group them. Any way of contributing counts. |
| 15 | Shape and vote | Dot-vote the top ideas on how much they help, who they include and how hard they are. |
| 10 | Next steps | What goes into the backlog, and when and how the group will hear back. |

Run by the group's own staff with a facilitator; breaks every 25 minutes; materials sent three working days before.

## Design crit (30 to 45 minutes)

| Minutes | Part | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Set the scene | What the design is for, who it's for, and what kind of feedback is wanted. |
| 20 | Look and respond | What works, what's confusing, what's missing. Feedback is on the work, never the person. |
| 10 | Decide | Agree the top three changes and who will make them; credit everyone who contributed. |

Designs by under 18s are shared without names, faces or school logos.

## Design routes

- **UI and interaction design:** Sketch a better screen on paper or in any tool, critique the tooltips and explainer, or test how controls behave with a keyboard or touch. Post a photo or file of your sketch in an Idea issue, with no faces, names or school logos.
- **UX and service design:** Map how you would really use Amplify, from hearing about it to taking part, and mark where it breaks. Use the journey template in docs/research/session-guides.md, or describe it in an Idea issue.
- **Graphic design and illustration:** Design a poster, sticker, badge or illustration using the six shapes and four hues in Brand & style. Attach it to an Idea issue under an open licence (CC BY 4.0) so it can be used and credited.
- **Content design:** Rewrite a confusing paragraph in plain English, check a reading age, or suggest what to translate or sign first. Open an Idea issue with the screen name, the old words and your version.
