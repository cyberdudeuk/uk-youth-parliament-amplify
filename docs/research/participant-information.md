## Why we are doing this research

Research tells us what to build next (the coming months) and where Amplify should go (the next few years). Design work with users makes it clearer, fairer and more accessible. Both are done in the open and are open to challenge.

## Our promise to you

- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, skip any question and withdraw what you said within 30 days, without giving a reason.
- Under 16s take part only with a parent's or carer's consent as well as their own. For 16 and 17 year olds we ask a parent or carer to be told, unless that would put the young person at risk.
- No one under 18 is ever in a session alone with a researcher. A parent, carer, teacher or youth worker is present or close by, and every facilitator has a background check (DBS, PVG or AccessNI).
- We recruit young people through trusted organisations and parents, never by messaging them directly online.
- Nothing is run through anyone who makes decisions about you: your answers are never shared with your school, employer or service.
- Contact details stay in the private form service and are deleted when you leave the panel or after 12 months without contact. They are never put on GitHub, on the portal or in any report.
- Everyone is thanked at the same rate, with a voucher that does not affect benefits, and travel and support costs are paid up front.
- Interpreters (BSL, ISL, Welsh, Gaelic) and other support are booked before a session is offered. You can answer by speaking, typing, drawing, signing or with a supporter.
- You see what we concluded, in a format that works for you, before anyone else does.
- If you tell us something that makes us worried about your safety, we will follow safeguarding procedures and tell you what we are doing.

## What happens in a session

- **Interviews** (30 to 45 minutes, online, by phone or in person): A conversation about how you speak up, what helps and what gets in the way. Shorter for younger people, always with a trusted adult nearby.
- **Usability testing** (30 to 45 minutes): You try real tasks while thinking aloud. We test the portal, not you. There are no wrong answers. You can stop at any time.
- **Accessibility testing with your own set-up** (45 to 60 minutes): You use Amplify with your own assistive technology, settings or language. Interpreters and support are booked before the session is offered.
- **Tree testing** (10 minutes, in your own time): You say where you would look for things in a text-only menu. Works on a phone. No sign-in.
- **Card sorting** (15 minutes, in your own time): You group topics in the way that makes sense to you. Can be done on paper in a class or youth group.
- **Content testing** (15 to 20 minutes): You mark words and sentences that help or confuse. Great for a class or youth council session.
- **Surveys** (5 minutes): Short questions you answer when it suits you. Anonymous, with no free-text field that asks who you are.
- **Diary study** (5 minutes a week for a month): You note when you used Amplify and what happened. 16 and over, or younger with a parent's or carer's agreement.
- **Co-design workshops** (60 to 90 minutes with breaks): You design a feature with other users and the team. Run through schools, youth councils and youth groups, never one-to-one with an under 18.
- **Design crits** (30 to 45 minutes, or in your own time): You give feedback on screens, posters, illustrations or words, or bring your own designs. Feedback is on the work, never the person.
- **Benchmark questions** (2 minutes): Ten standard questions (the System Usability Scale) so we can tell if Amplify gets easier to use. For 16 and over. Try it below: the score is worked out on your device and nothing is sent.

## What we keep

We take notes using a session code, never a name. Recordings are made only with consent and deleted within 30 days; notes are deleted after 6 months. Findings are published as themes and totals only.

## Taking part as a group

Schools, youth councils and youth groups can take part as a group: one adult registers and tells us roughly how many and what ages. We then send the participant information to that adult, and sessions run in your setting under your safeguarding policy.

Contact is by form only: no email address is published. Everything posted on GitHub is public, so never post names, schools or contact details.
