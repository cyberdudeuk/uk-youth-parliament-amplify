# Consent forms

Generated from `data/research.json` by `tools/make_docs.py`; edit that, not this file. The portal shows the same content on its Research & design screen.

Read out and shared in advance, in plain English, easy read or BSL/ISL as needed. Consent can be given by speaking, signing, writing or ticking, and is checked again before any clip is reused.

## For the young person

- [ ] I know what this research is about and what I will be asked to do.
- [ ] I know I can stop at any time, skip questions, and change my mind within 30 days.
- [ ] I know my name will not be used in anything published.
- [ ] I agree to take part.
- [ ] I agree to being recorded (optional: you can take part without this).

## For a parent or carer (required for under 16s)

- [ ] I have read (or had read to me) the information about this research.
- [ ] I understand my child can stop at any time, and that they can withdraw within 30 days.
- [ ] I understand a trusted adult will be present or close by throughout, and facilitators are background-checked.
- [ ] I consent to my child taking part.
- [ ] I consent to the session being recorded (optional).
