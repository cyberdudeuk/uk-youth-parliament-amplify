# Screener and quotas

Generated from `data/research.json` by `tools/make_docs.py`; edit that, not this file. The portal shows the same content on its Research & design screen.

Asked in a form that keeps contact details private. No question on GitHub ever asks who someone is.

## Questions

1. **Which best describes you?** A young person; a parent or carer; a teacher or tutor; a youth worker or group leader; a coding or STEM club leader; other.
2. **Age band (young people only)** 11 to 13; 14 to 15; 16 to 17; 18 to 25.
3. **Nation** England; Scotland; Wales; Northern Ireland; prefer not to say.
4. **Which activities would you like to try?** Interviews; testing; accessibility testing; short tasks in your own time; surveys; diary; co-design; design crits.
5. **Do you use assistive technology or a sign language? (optional)** No; screen reader; magnification or large text; voice or switch; BSL; ISL; other; prefer not to say.
6. **What would make taking part easier? (optional)** A time of day; captions; an interpreter; Welsh, Gaelic or Irish; breaks; questions in advance; phone or in person; a supporter.
7. **How should we contact you, or the adult registering for you?** Asked only in the private form, never on GitHub.

## Signing up

Signing up for research is paused. Before anyone your age takes part, Amplify needs a named safeguarding lead (the adult responsible for keeping you safe) and a written plan for what happens if something worries us (readiness gate G06, open items O12 and O14). Until both are in place we are not asking anyone to sign up, so please do not post that you want to take part. If you already took part, you can still withdraw what you said at any time with the contact form: give only your session code.

Contact is by form only: no email address is published. Everything posted on GitHub is public, so never post names, schools or contact details.
