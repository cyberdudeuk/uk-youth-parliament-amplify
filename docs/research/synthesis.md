## Steps

1. Write up within two weeks of the last session in a round.
2. Group notes into themes; count sessions per theme; rate severity (blocks, slows, annoys).
3. Fix anything that excludes a group first.
4. Turn each theme into a backlog item or a change, with the finding's id.
5. Show participants what we concluded, in their format, before publishing.
6. Publish the summary here, update You said, we did, and close the loop with the groups who took part.

## Benchmark

System Usability Scale by John Brooke (1986), free to use with attribution. Odd statements score the answer minus 1; even statements score 5 minus the answer (answers 1 to 5). Add the ten and multiply by 2.5 for a score from 0 to 100. Scores are compared between rounds, not against a pass mark. Published research puts the average across many products at about 68.
