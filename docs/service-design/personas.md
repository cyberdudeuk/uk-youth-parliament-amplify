> **Status:** Proto-personas and assumed journeys, written from the owner's requests, published guidance and the portal's own safeguards. They have not been tested with users yet: research Round 1 (January to February 2027) tests them, and they will change.

## Young person, 11 to 15

*At secondary school; uses a phone more than a laptop; may be a young carer, care-experienced, d/Deaf or disabled, or speak Welsh, Gaelic or Irish at home.*

**Goal:** Find out how to make my voice heard on something I care about, safely.

**Needs:** Plain words at a reading age of 11 to 13; Routes that fit my age and nation; To know who to talk to if I'm worried

**Pain points:** Jargon about Parliament; Not knowing what is safe to share; Many services are not for under 16s

**Kept safe by:** No sign-in, no personal data, helplines first; contributions through a trusted adult under 13.

**Starts at:** home

**Uses:** home, route, state, issues, care, stem

## Young person, 16 to 25

*At college, university, work or looking for work; may vote in Scottish or Welsh elections already; may be NEET.*

**Goal:** Take part directly: campaign, contribute and build skills I can show employers.

**Needs:** Real actions, not just information; Recognition for what I do; Ways in that fit around work or study

**Pain points:** Tokenistic consultation; Not hearing what changed; Unpaid time

**Kept safe by:** Clear rules on what GitHub makes public; ambassador and research promises.

**Starts at:** home

**Uses:** route, hub, kit, involve, research, ambassadors, stem

## Parent, guardian or carer

*Has parental responsibility; may be helping a child under 13 or under 16 use services that are age-limited.*

**Goal:** Support my child to speak up and keep them safe while they do.

**Needs:** To know what the portal asks of my child; How to act on their behalf; Safeguarding and helplines

**Pain points:** Not understanding the online safety rules; Worry about personal data; Time

**Kept safe by:** Age and access rules, acting on behalf, and consent for under 16s in research.

**Starts at:** home

**Uses:** home, trust, care, research, launch

## Youth worker or group leader

*Runs a youth club, uniformed group or youth council; works to a safeguarding policy.*

**Goal:** Plan sessions that help my group be heard, and run them safely.

**Needs:** Ready-to-run sessions; Inclusion for every young person; Safe outside activities

**Pain points:** No time to prepare; Resources that assume one nation; Unclear safeguarding for online activity

**Kept safe by:** Sessions run in the group's setting under its policy; background checks for visitors.

**Starts at:** learn

**Uses:** learn, route, incl, communities, care, campaign

**Needs:** Lessons that fit a period; Sourced facts; Something for every ability

## Coding or STEM club leader or mentor

*Runs a Code Club, CoderDojo or school club; wants real projects, especially for girls and young women.*

**Goal:** Give my club a real open-source project with professional practice to learn from.

**Needs:** Good first issues; Clear rules for under 18s on GitHub; A DevOps path

**Pain points:** Toy projects; Safety on public platforms; Few role models

**Kept safe by:** No personal data on GitHub; 13+ with parental agreement; reviews before anything goes live.

**Starts at:** stem

**Uses:** stem, involve, learn, health, research

## Councillor, policy maker, researcher or journalist

*Works with or reports on young people's participation; needs to cite sources.*

**Goal:** Understand youth voice across the UK and represent it accurately.

**Needs:** Sources and dates for every fact; Approved descriptions; Pan-UK picture

**Pain points:** Unsourced claims; England-only material; Confusing official and unofficial

**Kept safe by:** Unofficial status stated everywhere; never identify a young person.

**Starts at:** sources

**Uses:** sources, latest, policy, media, track, about

## d/Deaf or disabled young person (any age)

*Uses BSL or ISL, a screen reader, magnification, switch access or simpler language; may have a supporter.*

**Goal:** Use every part of the portal and take part on equal terms.

**Needs:** Keyboard and screen reader access; Sign-language and captioned routes; Explainers that do not rely on hover

**Pain points:** Tooltips that only work with a mouse; Interpreters as an afterthought; Forms that assume writing

**Kept safe by:** Interpreters booked first; any modality; supporters welcome.

**Starts at:** home

**Uses:** trust, help, research, care, feedback
