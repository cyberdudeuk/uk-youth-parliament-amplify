# Amplify campaign design brief: v2

How the UK Parliament Week launch collateral was redesigned. It draws on award-winning campaigns for young people, girls in STEM and youth democracy, and stays inside Amplify's own Brand & style guide.

Prepared 16 November 2026. Status: draft for the owner's review.

## 1. What the research found

| Campaign (recognition) | What worked | What Amplify takes from it |
|---|---|---|
| **Give an X**, UK youth voter registration, 2024 (reportedly more than 100,000 under-25s registered in 7 days, though not attributable to the campaign alone) | Bold graphics with a Gen Z sensibility. A casual, direct voice shaped by youth steering groups so it never talked down. The best asset said "Politics isn't about them, it's about you." | Type-led layouts. Copy that is about **you**. A ring drawn round a key word, like marking a ballot. |
| **Bite Back** brand by Wolff Olins (Third Sector Awards, Brand Development 2024). **#CommercialBreak** (Sheila McKechnie Award 2025) | The brand is a tool young people use to express themselves and protest: stickers, placards and pins, carried into schools by youth activists. Playful, confrontational wordplay. | A **sticker sheet** and **placard-style posters** built from Amplify's six shapes, which young people can use and remix. |
| **This Girl Can**, Sport England (Cannes Lions, D&AD, Creative Circle) | Real people instead of idealised ones. Short mantras. An app that let women make their own posters (nearly 40,000 made). | **Mantras** ("Girls code. Girls engineer. Girls ship.") and a **write-your-own poster** for UK Parliament Week groups. |
| **Always #LikeAGirl** (Cannes PR Lions Grand Prix 2015) | Built on the insight that girls' confidence drops at puberty. Turned a limiting phrase into a confident one. Target audience 10 to 24. | Confident, declarative statements about girls in tech. Never deficit framing. |
| **She Can STEM**, Ad Council (girls aged 11 to 15) | Role models, so girls can picture themselves in STEM. "She can STEM. So can you." | **Roles as identities.** The Government Digital and Data poster names real job titles and ends with "You." |
| Gen Z design and short-video practice (TikTok creative guidance, 2026 trend reports) | Bold expressive type. Authenticity over polish ("imperfect by design"). Younger and older Gen Z respond to different registers. Hook in the first 3 seconds. Captions at 5 to 10 words a second. Vertical 9:16. About 15 seconds. | **Two registers** (loud for 11 to 15, clean for 16 to 25 and adults), a **15-second vertical film** with kinetic captions, and slight rotation on stickers for a hand-made feel. |
| Girlguiding Girls' Attitudes Survey (a youth advocate panel shapes it) | Young people set the questions and the themes. | Every asset is open to remix (CC BY 4.0 under the portal's own rules) and invites ideas back through the contact form. |

## 2. Amplify's brand rules (unchanged, and now applied more fully)

From the portal's Brand & style guide:

- **Hue carries meaning.** Participation is 175, Equality 145, Education 300, Empowerment 70. Caution is 25, for failure states only and never decorative.
- **Six primitives only:** circle, ring, square, diamond, bar, triangle. No icon sets, no emoji.
- **Type.** Display headlines are heavy with tight tracking. Labels, data and provenance use Space Mono. Body copy is legible, never below 12.5px.
- **Surface.** Liquid glass: a 155° film gradient at 3 to 10%, a 1px hairline, an inset top sheen, radius 16 to 24.
- **Motion** is ambient, never required, and stops under reduced motion. Nothing is conveyed only by motion.
- **Voice.** Say the number, then what it means. Second person, active voice, British English. No exclamation marks, no hype, no emoji.
- **No photographs of young people** without consent. Shapes stand in for people.

### How each campaign thread maps to a hue

| Thread | Hue | Why |
|---|---|---|
| UK Parliament Week launch, "help build it", write your own poster | Participation 175 | A place where you act |
| STEM spotlight | Education 300 | Something you learn |
| Girls and young women in tech | Equality 145 | Who is reached |
| Government Digital and Data careers | Empowerment 70 | Gives leverage |

## 3. The campaign layer: what's new, built from brand pieces

1. **Aurora ground.** Soft, blurred fields of the thread's hues on ink. This is the guide's "drifting aurora", made static for print.
2. **Glass cards** follow the guide's surface recipe exactly.
3. **Die-cut stickers.** The six primitives with a white keyline, a word in Space Mono and up to 8° of rotation. This is the Bite Back and Gen Z hand-made layer, using only Amplify's own shapes.
4. **The ring mark.** A ring primitive drawn round one key word, the gesture of marking a ballot (the Give an X "about you" idea).
5. **Mantras and big numbers.** Display type at poster scale, then the number followed by what it means, with the source in Space Mono.
6. **Two registers.** *Loud* (ink ground, stickers, saturated) for 11 to 15 and social. *Clean* (paper ground, editorial, data-led) for 16 to 25, adults, print and email. This mirrors the portal's own "Explain for" age modes.

## 4. Accessibility and safety guardrails

- All text meets WCAG AA contrast (checked in code). The film has no flashing and captions are the content, so it works with no sound.
- The film stays under 3 words per second, below TikTok's 5 to 10 guide, so slower readers can follow. A still version ships with it.
- No photographs, no names and no personal data on the write-your-own poster. It asks what you'd change, not who you are.
- Posts are for adults, allies and 13+ accounts, as the portal's campaign kit sets out.

## 5. Open questions for the owner

- **Display face.** The live Brand & style screen names *Fraunces 900* for headlines, but the repository only ships Chivo, Atkinson Hyperlegible and Space Mono, and the live headings render in Chivo. This campaign uses **Chivo 900**, which matches what people actually see. Decide whether Fraunces should be added or the guide corrected.
