Equality, equity, diversity, inclusion, belonging

Recognition in this portal counts pledges, schools reached and briefings delivered — never follower counts, which reward the already-heard. Regional standing is normalised by population so smaller nations are not structurally bottom of the table.

Safeguarding by age band

Under-13s share only into groups they already belong to, are never publicly named, and have pledges confirmed by a named adult. 14–18 gains public sharing with first name and initial. Adult-to-young-person messaging is off by default in both.

Digital exclusion

Signing requires an email address and a confirmation click, which is itself an access barrier. Low-data mode, the offline pledge pack and youth-club terminals exist because the barrier is not evenly distributed.
Ethics & values

Four cornerstones, and everything in this portal sits on one of them

Campaigning is youth work, not marketing. The National Youth Work Curriculum sets out four cornerstones and a code of ethics for work with young people. They are the frame for this whole product: every screen answers to one of them, and the colour it carries tells you which.

Cornerstone one

Participation

Work with young people as partners and leaders in their own learning, so they gain influence over the issues they care about and a real route into democratic process.

Cornerstone two

Equality

For all young people. Respect difference, build connections between groups, and promote human rights, social justice and anti-oppressive practice — supporting and challenging young people as you go.

Cornerstone three

Education

Informal learning that complements school and college: about themselves, about others, about the issues that concern them, and about how to act in their own communities.

Cornerstone four

Empowerment

Build the skills and confidence to make decisions and act — on their own lives, on other people’s, on their communities — so young people take control and advocate for themselves.

Code of ethics · the non-negotiables

The Code sits above the campaign

The Youth Work Code of Ethics sets the values, behaviours and professional standards for safe, respectful and empowering work with young people. It was refreshed with youth workers and young people. Where a growth tactic and the Code disagree, the Code wins — which is why this portal counts pledges rather than followers and never asks a young person to post publicly to unlock anything.

Values are written down, not implied

The values and principles of youth work are set out in the Youth Work National Occupational Standards, for which the National Youth Agency is the Professional Statutory Regulatory Body in England. Anything a campaign asks an adult ally to do is checked against them first.

Rights, not favours

The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child sets out 54 articles including the right to express an opinion and be listened to, and the right to an education that lets a child fulfil their potential. A petition on political education is an article-by-article argument, not a request for goodwill.

The rules that bind every route
Political neutrality. The UKYP Rulebook requires MYPs to campaign non-partisanly. You represent your constituents, not a party.
A term runs April to March. Miss the local election window and the next one is up to two years away.
Elections are local. The National Youth Agency does not hold your area’s timetable — the local authority youth service or Regional Youth Work Unit does. Find yours on the local routes map.
Not elected is not excluded. Make Your Mark, local youth councils, consultations and the Youth Select Committee call for evidence are all open without a seat.

Six primitives only: circle, ring, square, diamond, bar, triangle. No illustrative icon sets, no emoji. A shape plus a cornerstone hue is the whole system, which is why it survives translation, Easy Read and high contrast.

Surface · liquid glass

One recipe, used everywhere: a 155° film gradient at 3–10% opacity, a 16–22px backdrop blur with 160% saturation, a 1px hairline border, and a 1px inset top sheen. Radius 16 for nested, 20–24 for cards. This card is the reference — the specular sweep you can see is the amp-glass class.

Motion & voice
Motion is ambient, never required. Drifting aurora, a 9s specular sweep, a breathing dot. Every animation dies under reduced motion, and nothing carries meaning that only motion conveys.
