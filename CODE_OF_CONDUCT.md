# Code of conduct

This portal is written for young people. Everyone taking part in issues, discussions and pull requests is expected to:

- be respectful, inclusive and patient, whatever someone's age, background or level of experience
- assume good intent and give feedback on the work, not the person
- never share personal data about anyone, and never anything that identifies a young person
- follow their own organisation's code of conduct where it applies

Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not accepted. Maintainers may hide, edit or remove contributions that break
this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

Raise a concern through the [contact form](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/uk-youth-parliament-amplify/issues/new?template=contact.yml).
No email address is published: contact is by form only.
